Privacy Policy

Rihla — Effective date: August 25, 2026

Overview

Rihla is a daily Islamic habit companion — prayer times, Qibla direction, Qur'an reading, and curated daily reflections. This policy explains what information Rihla collects, how it is used, and — just as importantly — what we deliberately do not do with it.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share your location, or any other personal data, with advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.
  • We do not run third-party ad networks inside the app.
  • We do not require an account to use the core app (prayer times, Qibla, Qur'an, daily tasks).

Location data

Prayer times and Qibla direction are calculated on your device using your device's GPS coordinates. This calculation happens locally and your precise location is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party.

Account & streak data (optional)

If you choose to create an account to back up your streak and progress across devices, we store your daily task-completion history and streak count, associated with your account identifier. This data is used solely to display your own progress back to you and is never shared externally.

Payments

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details — Google handles that transaction directly under its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Rihla is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
